# Security reports

Email reports to [pwdhash@nmic.app](mailto:pwdhash@nmic.app). Include the
platform, PwdHash version, and steps using synthetic inputs such as
`example.com`. Keep real passwords, generated credentials, signing material,
and private site addresses out of the report.

See the [privacy and compatibility sections](README.md#privacy-and-platform-behavior)
for the design limits of the legacy algorithm and platform clipboard behaviour.
